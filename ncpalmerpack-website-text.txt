--- FAQ ---

FAQ

Common Questions

Q: What products does Palmer Packaging supply?
A: Palmer Packaging supplies over 12,000 packaging products including corrugated boxes (RSC, FOL, die-cut, custom), stretch film, shrink wrap, poly and steel strapping, bubble wrap, foam, poly bags, packaging tape, edge protectors, kraft paper, mailing tubes, envelopes, janitorial supplies, and custom packaging solutions.

Q: Does Palmer Packaging offer custom stocking programs?
A: Yes. Palmer Packaging offers custom inventory stocking programs out of their Addison, IL warehouse. Your supplies stay stocked and ready without tying up your own warehouse space or capital. Neil Curtis will design a program around your usage patterns and delivery schedule.

Q: What areas does Palmer Packaging serve?
A: Palmer Packaging is headquartered in Addison, IL with distribution centers across the country. Their company-owned fleet delivers directly to Chicago and all surrounding suburbs — no third-party carriers, no delays. Products can also be shipped to customers outside the local delivery area.

Q: How do I get a packaging quote?
A: Contact Neil Curtis directly at (630) 628-6500 or neil@ncpalmerpack.com. He's available Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm CST and typically responds the same business day with competitive pricing.

Q: Does Palmer Packaging service and repair packaging equipment?
A: Yes. Palmer offers installation, repair, and preventive maintenance on strapping machines, stretch wrap equipment, tape dispensers, heat seal systems, and other packaging equipment. Neil can help evaluate your current setup and recommend upgrades when it makes sense.

Q: What makes Palmer Packaging different from other suppliers?
A: 35+ years of experience, below-retail distributor pricing, a company-owned delivery fleet serving Chicago and surrounding suburbs, 6 distribution centers, 12,000+ products in stock, and dedicated sales representatives like Neil Curtis who learn your operation and recommend packaging that actually fits your needs and your budget.
