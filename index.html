Solutions

Packaging Solutions for Every Need

Over 12,000 products in stock and ready for next day delivery — all priced below retail. From corrugated boxes to stretch film, Palmer carries everything you need, and Neil will make sure you're paying less than you are now.

Semi-auto and automatic strapping machines, stretch wrap equipment, tape dispensers, and heat seal systems — plus installation, repair, and preventive maintenance.

Protective Packaging

Bubble wrap, foam rolls and sheets, corner protectors, edge boards, void fill, and cushioning materials to prevent damage during shipping and handling.

Corrugated Boxes

Stocking Programs

Company-Owned Fleet

Janitorial & Facility Supplies

Trash liners, cleaning supplies, and facility maintenance products — everything you need to keep your warehouse and workspace running smoothly alongside your packaging operation.
